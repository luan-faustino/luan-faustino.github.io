About

I started in technology in 2022 and went through software development, IT support and infrastructure before moving into Information Security in 2024. That path gives me an end-to-end view of the environment, from servers and networks to detection and incident response.

I treat my own automations with the same security rigor I expect from the systems I protect: least privilege, secrets in a vault, audit logs and human approval for any change.
